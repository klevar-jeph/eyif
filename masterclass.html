Six Masterclasses — Pick One

Choose Your Breakout Session

Masterclasses run across Day 1 and Day 2 of the forum, all from 9:00 AM to 10:50 AM. Each delegate may attend only one — read the summaries below to find the session that best matches your goals and interests.
Tue 18 Aug 2026 · 9:00 AM – 10:50 AM
MC 1 Day 1
01
AI for Small Businesses: Automation

A hands-on intro to using AI tools for customer support, content creation, marketing, and scheduling — no technical background needed. Walk away with a shortlist of free tools and a framework for automating your biggest time-sinks.

Ideal for: small business owners, startups & freelancers.
Facilitator Halitta AI & Tech Ecosystem
MC 2 Day 1
02
From Idea to Brand: Creative Economy Business Models for Edo Creatives

Built for Edo State's creatives — learn how to move from a raw idea to a recognisable, income-generating brand. Explore real business models from productised services to licensing, and learn how to price and position your work.

Ideal for: designers, content creators, musicians & artists.
Facilitator Edo Pikin Comedian & Creative Host
MC 3 Day 1
03
Skill Stacking: How to Turn One Skill Into Seven Income Streams

Discover how a single skill — writing, design, or coding — can be repackaged into consulting, teaching, products, licensing, and content. Leave with a personalised skill-stacking map for your current skill set.

Ideal for: freelancers, young professionals & side-hustlers.
Facilitator Adeife Adeoye Founder, Remote WorkHer
MC 4 Day 1
04
Storytelling as Strategy: Using Film, Music & Art to Build Economic Power

Learn how storytellers across film, music, and visual art can use narrative as a deliberate strategy to build audiences, attract funding, and create lasting economic value. Includes case studies and a repeatable storytelling framework.

Ideal for: filmmakers, musicians, artists & content creators.
Facilitator Victor Sanchez Aghahowa Head of Production, West Africa, MultiChoice Africa
MC 5 Day 1
05
Social Enterprise & Measuring Impact: Building Ventures That Prove Their Worth

Build a venture that is both financially sustainable and socially impactful. Learn practical tools for impact measurement, how to build an impact framework, and how to tell your story with data that funders trust.

Ideal for: social entrepreneurs, nonprofit leaders & changemakers.
Facilitator Mrs Isimeme Whyte Founder / GMD, Genius Hub
MC 6 Day 2
06
Raising Capital in Nigeria: Grants, Angels & What Funders Actually Want

A practical, no-fluff guide to Nigeria's funding landscape — from grants and angel investors to what funders actually look for before writing a cheque. Learn how to position your business for capital, build a fundable pitch, and avoid the common mistakes that kill deals before they start.

Ideal for: founders, early-stage entrepreneurs & teams seeking funding.
Facilitator David Olaoluwa Adeleke Co-Founder & CEO, Zeeh Africa
